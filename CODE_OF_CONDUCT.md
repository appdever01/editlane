# Code of conduct

Editlane welcomes contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Be respectful in issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

Critique the work and explain your reasoning. Ask for context when something is unclear, give useful feedback, and respect another person's time and privacy.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, sexualized conduct, and publishing someone's private information are unacceptable. Personal attacks and repeated disruption of a discussion are also unacceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

For a public incident, notify [@appdever01](https://github.com/appdever01) in the affected conversation without repeating harmful or private material. For sensitive incidents that should not be posted publicly, use [GitHub's abuse-reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam); those reports are handled by GitHub.

The maintainer may ask for a change in behavior, remove content, close or lock a conversation, or restrict participation. The response depends on the severity and repetition of the conduct. Apply this policy consistently, including to maintainers.
